# Outbound Engine

> **Most AI cold-email tools start with the email. Outbound Engine starts with the prospect.**

An open-source framework for building research-driven, buyer-centric B2B outbound campaigns.

Instead of asking AI to "write a cold email," Outbound Engine works backwards from the prospect:

**Research → Evidence → Hypothesis → Message → QA**

The goal isn't to generate more emails.

The goal is to create messages that give a specific prospect a credible reason to respond.

---

## Why Outbound Engine?

Most AI-generated cold email follows a familiar pattern:

**Product → Pitch → Features → CTA**

Outbound Engine takes a different approach:

**Prospect → Business Trigger → Problem → Consequence → Future State → Credible Path → Conversation**

The prospect is the protagonist.

The sender's product, technology and company credentials are secondary.

---

## The Core Method

### 1. Research

Understand the prospect and account before writing.

Look for:

- Role and responsibilities
- Company context
- Business model
- Growth signals
- Strategic initiatives
- Recent business events
- Observable customer or operational issues

### 2. Evidence

Separate what is known from what is inferred.

Every insight should be classified as:

- **Observed fact** — directly supported by evidence
- **Reasonable inference** — plausible interpretation
- **Unknown** — cannot be established confidently

Never present an inference as a fact.

### 3. Hypothesis

Connect the evidence into a business hypothesis:

**Trigger → Current State → Friction → Consequence → Future State**

The objective is to identify a problem the prospect may genuinely care about.

### 4. Message

Turn the hypothesis into a concise conversation starter.

The default initial email uses:

1. Context / Trigger
2. Current State / Problem
3. Future State + Relevant Proof
4. Interest-Based CTA
5. Optional PS

The target is normally **4–5 sentences**.

### 5. QA

Every email is evaluated against a 50-point quality framework covering:

- Trigger relevance
- Problem specificity
- Customer centricity
- Future-state clarity
- Proof relevance
- Personalization
- CTA quality
- Brevity
- Human tone
- Factual confidence

**Target: 40/50 minimum.**

If an email is generic, unsupported, feature-first or otherwise fails the quality gate, it should be regenerated.

---

## Sequence Strategy

Outbound Engine treats a sequence as a progression rather than six versions of the same email.

| Touch | Strategic purpose |
|---|---|
| 1 | Relevance |
| 2 | Reframe |
| 3 | Proof |
| 4 | Insight |
| 5 | Objection removal |
| 6 | Decision |

Every follow-up should introduce something new.

No:

> "Just following up."

Instead, each touch should add an observation, consequence, proof point, insight, objection response or decision path.

---

## Personalization Hierarchy

Not all personalization is equally valuable.

Outbound Engine prioritizes:

**Business Trigger → Role Context → Company Problem → Human Detail**

A recent strategic event is more valuable than a generic compliment.

The objective is not to make an email *look* personalized.

The objective is to establish a genuine business reason for the prospect to care.

---

## Multi-Channel Outreach

Where appropriate, the framework can coordinate:

- Cold email
- Cold calling
- LinkedIn

The channels should reinforce recognition without simply repeating the same message.

For high-value accounts, concentrated multi-touch "burst" activity can be used when appropriate.

---

## Accuracy First

Outbound Engine does not permit fabricated:

- Customers
- Case studies
- Metrics
- Results
- Funding
- Hiring
- Partnerships
- Technology usage
- Personal details
- Business events

When certainty is unavailable, the framework uses calibrated language such as:

> "It looks like..."

> "Teams in this position often..."

> "You may be seeing..."

The system should never manufacture credibility.

---

## The 500-Company Test

Before approving an email, ask:

> **Could this exact email be sent to 500 other companies without changing anything?**

If yes, it fails.

Regenerate.
